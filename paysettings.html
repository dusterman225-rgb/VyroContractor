Get paid in crypto

Customers pay from their Vyro app by scanning a QR code on your invoice. The money goes straight to your wallet. Vyro never holds it. Vyro's fee is 2% of each payment, taken out of the payment automatically.

Use a wallet you control. Payments sent to a wrong address cannot be reversed. After saving, check the first and last 4 characters match your wallet app.

How a payment works

1. Open an invoice and tap Pay with Vyro. 2. Pick the coin and show the QR to your customer. 3. They open Vyro, tap Send, and scan it. 4. Vyro checks the blockchain and marks the invoice paid by itself. You never type these payments in.
